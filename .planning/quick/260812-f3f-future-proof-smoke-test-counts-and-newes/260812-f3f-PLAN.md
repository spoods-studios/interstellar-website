Facts that constrain the implementation:

- Two devlog entries carry date 2026-08-11. `src/lib/entry-order.ts`'s
  `compareNewestFirst` breaks that tie by putting the `milestone-addendum`-tagged
  entry first, so the newest entry is the addendum, not the alphabetically- or
  timestamp-later one. A date-only derivation picks the wrong post and the
  neighbour-nav assertion fails.
- `src/lib/entry-order.ts` imports nothing and loads under bare Node.
  `src/lib/devlog-meta.ts` does NOT — its extensionless `./title-from-h1` /
  `./entry-order` imports raise ERR_MODULE_NOT_FOUND outside Vite (verified this
  session). Import the comparator, mirror devlog-meta's tiny date/addendum
  extraction locally, and say so in a comment.
- `entryDate` falls back to the filename's leading `YYYY-MM-DD` when frontmatter
  carries no `date:`; the manifesto has no frontmatter at all and depends on that
  fallback.
- `status: draft` hides an entry site-wide (D-30 `isVisible`), so drafts must be
  excluded from every derived count or the expectation will exceed what builds.

  (`hero-assets.ts` -> `entry.assetImports?.[0]`), NOT from the `hero_visual`

  Derive heroes from the first Markdown image reference in each body.
