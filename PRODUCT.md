# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Space-sim players, chiefly Kerbal Space Program and Orbiter fans, arriving from
Discord, Reddit or a shared link and deciding whether Setare Aerospace is worth
following before Early Access.

## Product Purpose

The public face of Interstellar Engine and Setare Aerospace: a feature showcase
of the game in its current state and of what is planned and upcoming. Success is
a visitor who understands what the game is and joins the Discord.

## Positioning

Orbiter-grade physics (N-body, relativistic effects) with KSP-style
accessibility, a real-scale solar system built from measured data, and an
integrated knowledge base that teaches real orbital mechanics.

## Operating Context

- Solo developer, Spoods Studios. PC (Steam) Early Access planned at $15 with
  free content updates; no Steam page or Patreon is live yet.
- The Discord invite is the only live call to action.
- Progress ships milestone by milestone (Era 0 done, Era 1 in progress); the
  roadmap pages track the arc.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Astro 7 static site on GitHub Pages under `/interstellar-website`; no backend.
- No cookies; analytics stay cookieless (GoatCounter) or absent.
- A deployed URL is permanent: removed pages redirect.
- Screenshots and footage are engine gameplay captures only (D-CF); no mocked
  or generated imagery (D-K).

## Brand Commitments

- Names: Interstellar Engine (the engine), Setare Aerospace (the game), Spoods
  Studios (the studio).
- Frame other space games (KSP, KSA) with respect, never comparison marketing
  (D-L).
- Copy uses the technical devlog voice in the studio `vault/devlog/VOICE.md`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Engine captures produced from the current build (to be captured for the
  showcase); four older hero PNGs in `assets/` (M0.7, M0.8, M1.1, M1.2).
- Roadmap pages in `roadmap/` and `pages/roadmap.md`.
- No testimonials, press, player counts or release dates exist; never invent
  them.

## Product Principles

- Show the real thing: every image and clip is engine output.
- Honest about state: current build and planned work are labelled as such.
- Physics credibility first, spectacle in service of it.
