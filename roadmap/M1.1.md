## Phase 48: Rigid-Body Attitude Kernel — ✅ 2026-07-14

**Goal:** standalone 6DOF rigid-body attitude kernel — float64 quaternion +
body-frame angular velocity + principal-moment inertia tensor, integrated
deterministically on a fixed step, validated in isolation with a Dzhanibekov
intermediate-axis-flip math-lock fixture. No worker/render dependency, no
thrust, no RCS, no input handling.

**What it delivered:**
- `det_sin_cos` — a bounded-domain (64π) halving + 5-term Horner +
  double-angle-reconstruction trig primitive, `+−×÷` only, mirroring
  `det_stumpff`'s quartering; measured max error 2.743e-13, locked at
  1.5e-12; loud NaN rejection beyond the domain bound or on non-finite
  input, never a silently-wrong value
- `RigidBodyProps`/`RigidBodyState` + `rigid_body_step` — the DLM/
  NO_SQUISH exact-axis-rotation 5-stage symmetric splitting
  (`R3·R2·R1·R2·R3`) inside a KDK torque wrap; torque=0 is a structural
  no-op via a `has_torque` presence flag, mirroring `OblatenessProps::has_j2`
  — never compute-then-add-zero
- The stage order, kick placement, and in-plane sign convention were
  empirically arbitrated by an independent RK4 integration of Euler's
  equations — the correct sign diverged from RK4 by 1.36e-7 over 2s, the
  flipped sign by 9.60e-1 (seven orders of magnitude), while both
  conserved `|L|` and energy exactly (conservation alone cannot catch a
  sign error)
- `validate_rigid_body_props` — throwing validation (finite + strictly
  positive principal moments), never called on the hot step
- A Dzhanibekov math-lock (a four-part stack plus a calibrated band): the
  Berkeley perturbed-IC fixture (I={1,2,3}, ω₀={1e-4,1,1e-4}, dt=0.1s,
  600s horizon) reproduces 17 intermediate-axis flips, first at 18.9s
  (locked band [15.12, 22.68]s); `|L|` conserved to 6.11e-14 (locked
  3.0e-13), energy bounded to 9.01e-4 (locked 5.0e-3); same-binary golden
  at three checkpoints; forward/negate-ω/forward/negate-ω round trip
  residual 1.36e-9 (locked 7.0e-9)
- Full suite green at phase exit: Release 678/678, Debug 674/674; both
  libm scans clean (the attitude and det-math translation units, `det_sin_cos`/
  `det_sqrt` whitelist only); determinism (`nbody_force.cpp`) diff empty
  across the whole phase

**Key decisions:** exact-axis-rotation splitting over semi-implicit Euler —
by-construction `|L|`/energy behavior and exact time-reversibility,
renormalization as hygiene rather than correction; a principal-moments-only
inertia contract — zero runtime diagonalization, a full 3×3 tensor is
deferred to a part-based-craft milestone; `I_body` fixed for all of M1.1,
consistent with the point-mass fuel model; hand-rolled quaternion algebra
via `det_sqrt`, no GLM quat ops; flip timing locked as a calibrated ±20%
band rather than an exact value — robust to benign refactors while still
catching separatrix-distorting regressions the same way the golden catches
exact-bit ones.

**Deviations:** none — both plans report zero deviations from plan; the
recommended sign convention passed the RK4 arbiter on the first
implementation attempt, so the plan's sign-flip contingency was never
triggered.

## Phase 49: Tsiolkovsky Thrust & Fuel Kernel — ✅ 2026-07-14

**Goal:** standalone momentum-balance thrust kernel where an analog throttle
drives thrust and fuel depletion together; a full burn matches closed-form
Tsiolkovsky Δv exactly; the Stanford AA284a gravity-loss closed form locks
alongside as a second empirical anchor; empty tank cuts thrust to zero. No
n-body wiring (Phase 51), no player input (Phase 52), no HUD (Phase 53).

**What it delivered:**
- `det_log(double) noexcept` — IEEE-754 bit-seed exponent split + a
  sqrt(2)-centered 10-term atanh series, libm-free, accurate to ~1 ULP
  (measured max abs error 8.882e-16) over a `[2^-7, 2^7]` plausibility
  window; bit-exact `+0.0` at `x == 1.0` for the already-empty-tank edge
- `thrust_force.{hpp,cpp}`: `ThrustProps{v_e_mps, thrust_max_n,
  m_dry_kg}` / `ThrustStep{dv_mps, m_new_kg, burned_out, burn_frac}`,
  `thrust_step` implementing an analytic log kick
  (`dm = u·mdot_max·dt`, `dv = v_e·det_log(m0/m1)`) so a full burn
  telescopes to closed-form Tsiolkovsky exactly regardless of step count
  — the mass-sampling clock mismatch a research note had flagged is dead
  by construction, not by tolerance
- Exact partial burn at tank-empty: mass lands bit-exactly on `m_dry_kg`,
  no whole-substep clamp, no stranded fuel; full-burn Δv hits the closed
  form exactly at burnout
- `burn_active(props, m_kg, throttle)` — the one engine-wide "burning"
  predicate, reused as `thrust_step`'s own gate and named as the shared
  predicate for Phase 51's warp gate and Phase 53's HUD
- Derived accessors enforcing the momentum-balance identity by
  construction: `mdot_max_kg_s = thrust_max_n/v_e_mps`,
  `isp_seconds = v_e_mps/G0_STANDARD` — an inconsistent `(T, ṁ, Isp)`
  triple is unrepresentable
- Calibrate-then-lock full-burn Tsiolkovsky regression at two substep
  counts: measured 1.819e-12 @ N=960, 1.910e-11 @ N=3840, locked as
  `kFullBurnTol(N) = 2.5e-14·N`
- A test-local vertical-ascent harness driving the real `thrust_step`
  kernel against an independent long-double oracle, reproducing the
  Stanford AA284a gravity-loss closed form (`v = v_e·ln(Mᵢ/M) − g·t_burn`)
  to a measured 9.91918e-12 @ N=1921, locked at `5.0e-11`
- A documented decision pinning the analytic-log-kick formulation, naming
  both regressions as validating evidence and explicitly deferring
  impulse-at-kick KDK placement to Phase 51
- A fix from code review: a `dv_from_mass_ratio` helper peels exact
  factors of 128 off the high mass before any ratio reaches `det_log`,
  closing a silent-NaN path the per-substep (as opposed to total wet/dry)
  mass ratio could hit under a large single substep against a
  high-mass-ratio craft
- Another review fix: a symmetric non-finite gate across `dt`,
  `throttle`, and `m_kg` — a non-finite input now takes the same
  structural no-op passthrough instead of producing a contract-violating
  `burn_frac` or a silent NaN
- Phase-end gate: Release 698/698, Debug 694/694; libm scans on the
  det-math and thrust translation units clean; `nbody_force.cpp`
  byte-untouched across the whole phase

**Key decisions:** an analytic log kick over midpoint/endpoint force-kick
discretization so the full-burn regression carries zero discretization
tolerance; a new `det_log` primitive required rather than reusing/
relaxing the libm ban, single-branch sqrt(2)-centered atanh form over a
Cephes-style dual branch; Δv-impulse is the kernel's canonical public
output, not an `a = F/m` acceleration surface, so Phase 51 applies it as a
velocity increment at the kick boundary; linear analog throttle with
constant Isp and no kernel-enforced min-throttle floor, Isp degradation and
throttle deadbands deferred to a post-M1.1 realism pass; `v_e` canonical
with Isp/mdot derived so the momentum-balance identity is structural, not
validated; a scalar 1D kernel with no frame assumption, direction ownership
left to Phase 51's rotation-conversion seam; wet mass canonical, fuel
derived as `m_kg − m_dry_kg`; the gravity-loss harness stays entirely
test-side, zero production surface.

**Deviations:** none from the phase plan. The code review found one
Critical (a silent NaN from an unguarded per-substep mass ratio) and two
Warnings (a non-finite `dt` producing an out-of-contract `burn_frac`; `dt`'s
non-finite inputs gated less strictly than `throttle`'s) — user-approved
fixes landed in-phase, zero deferred weakness.

## Phase 50: RCS Mixer Kernel — ✅ 2026-07-20

**Goal:** build a standalone, data-driven fixed-geometry RCS
thruster-allocation kernel — a pseudo-inverse allocation matrix mapping
a body-frame wrench (force + torque) to non-negative per-thruster
duties, with the zero-cross-coupling property proven as the exact
`B·B⁺ = I₆` linear-algebra identity rather than a closed-loop
convergence check. No controller, no worker wiring, no player input this
phase.

**What it delivered:**
- `RcsProps`/`RcsAllocation`/`RcsMixResult` + `validate_rcs_props` /
  `build_rcs_allocation` / `rcs_mix` (`rcs_mixer.{hpp,cpp}`):
  cold-path Gauss-Jordan pseudo-inverse precompute (`B⁺ =
  Bᵀ·(B·Bᵀ)⁻¹`, row-only partial pivoting), hot-path GEMV → opposing-pair
  non-negativity split → uniform saturation scale-down → paired-order
  achieved-wrench readback
- Zero-coupling proven as the exact `B·B⁺ = I₆` identity, locked at
  `== 0.0` on the canonical 12-thruster cube across the unconstrained,
  saturated, and pair-split branches alike, plus a golden `==` table of
  the cube's 72 `B⁺` entries and build determinism
- General-geometry lane: an 8-thruster asymmetric fixture (8 distinct
  `thrust_max` values, no opposing pairs) locked against an analytic
  N-aware ULP bound (`κ(M) = 69.07`, ~17–27× measured headroom), and a
  dual-fixture calibration confirming the rank-pivot relative-tolerance
  constant sits at the log-midpoint between the roundoff floor and the
  smallest valid pivot
- A zero-alloc gate (standalone executable, work-fired assertion); a
  non-finite-input propagate+flag contract; libm-free but for one
  cold-path `det_sqrt` (confirmed by symbol scan)
- Code review: fixed same-day — a finite wrench could overflow internally
  to NaN duties while the input-side non-finite flag stayed false; a new
  output-side flag completes the contract. Two Info fixes (named
  `epsilon()` constant, explicit `axis_name` case); one Info (public
  `RcsAllocation` aggregate) disposed `wontfix`
- Phase-end two-lane gate: Release 722/722, Debug 718/718;
  `nbody_force.cpp` byte-untouched

**Key decisions:** the right pseudo-inverse over SVD — `B` is 6×N full row
rank, so the closed-form 6×6 solve is exact and needs no iterative
eigen-solve; an opposing-pair split for non-negativity, built from the
same table the achieved-wrench readback uses — a single source of truth
for both prevents the split and the cancellation grouping from silently
diverging; uniform 1/max(u) saturation on **post-split** duties; row-only
pivoting so rank rejection names the deficient physical axis;
asymmetric-geometry fixtures added alongside the symmetric cube so
idealized-only tests can't mask structural bugs (an M0.5 precedent).

**Deviations:** the plan's Test 6 saturation scale was internally
inconsistent — it stated `2^17 N` force with "unconstrained max duty
256" but computed the scale from the pre-split max (`1/256`) rather than
the post-split max the algorithm saturates on; corrected
to `scale_factor == 2⁻⁹ (1/512)`, the value the specified algorithm
produces. Case 7's commands were built as `w = B·u₀` for a
strictly-positive in-row-space `u₀` rather than pure unit axes, since an
8-thruster no-pair fixture's row space barely pierces the positive
orthant (Stiemke's lemma) and pure axis commands drove duties negative,
pulling the split path into what was meant to be a pure-rounding
accuracy lane.

## Phase 51: n-Body Integration Hookup — ✅ 2026-07-20

**Goal:** thread the Phase 48/49 attitude and thrust kernels into the locked
n-body core through the existing kick seams — single craft on the
`FeelOnly` test-particle slot, thrust via one documented
`R(q)*F_body -> F_inertial` conversion seam, burn/warp exclusion including a
craft warp-drift path that closes an M0.7 test-particle-in-warp deferral,
fuel-depletion step order pinned and math-locked, craft-absent runs
byte-identical to the pre-M1.1 close.

**What it delivered:**
- `rotate_body_to_inertial` — expanded quaternion sandwich, pure `+-x`, no
  libm; the thrust axis pinned to +X body; tested worker-free against
  fixed known orientations only, isolated from any live worker context
- `CraftConfig` on `PhysicsWorker::Config` (empty = feature absent); craft
  rides the existing `FeelOnly` test-particle slot with mass/fuel/throttle/
  attitude in a `CraftState` sidecar; the craft-absent invariant proven by
  golden locks staying green craft-absent PLUS a structural-skip counter
- The fuel-depletion order: a symmetric Delta-v split (`half = 0.5*dv`
  before dispatch, `second = dv - half` after) bracketing the tier
  dispatch at the OUTERMOST fixed-step boundary, never inside the shared
  force callback; mass commits at the first half-kick — "mass leads,
  velocity splits"; a 4-case lock stack (bitwise order tripwire,
  Tsiolkovsky-through-the-seam at 16 ULP vs a measured 2.95 ULP,
  same-binary mid-burn golden, determinism)
- Closes the M0.7 test-particle-in-warp deferral for the single-craft
  case: a coasting craft drifts each warp step on a Kepler-universal conic
  about its mass-dominant body (zero new math); dominance re-keys every
  warp step so sphere-of-influence crossings stay warpable; three
  calibrate-then-lock divergence fixtures vs flown truth over 7200 s
  (quiet-helio 10.37 m -> 50 m, transfer 420 m -> 2100 m, moon-perturbed
  2606 m -> 13000 m)
- Burn/warp exclusion: entry clamps `time_scale` below `warp_in_scale`
  while `burn_active` (a throttled log line, no cooldown); a burn due
  mid-warp latches — exits via the existing warp-exit-session export path
  and ignites in the same iteration; zero-thrust-in-warp enforced
  structurally, by Debug assert, and by bitwise tripwire
- Craft flight block (present, mass, fuel, throttle, burn-active, attitude
  quaternion) published unconditionally per snapshot under the existing
  publish-poison finiteness guard — Phase 53 reads it with no further
  worker touch
- Determinism (`nbody_force.cpp`) byte-untouched phase-wide; worker-TU
  libm gate stays exactly `{cos, sqrt}`; full suite 731/731 -> 760/760
  (Release), 756/756 (Debug) across the five plans

**Key decisions:** the fuel-depletion order (symmetric split, mass-leads,
outermost boundary, full lock stack); the warp deferral closed narrowly
(single-craft conic drift, dominance re-key, calibrate-then-lock
divergence bound); the burn/warp gate (latch-export-ignite, clamp plus
throttled log); the declared-config presence scaffold; a static
pre-Phase-52 attitude source and the +X thrust axis; the unconditional
flight-block publish.

**Deviations:** none functional across all five plans — every plan
executed RED then GREEN as written. Mechanical notes only: `Vec3f64`
exposes no `operator+=`, so kick-site writes use plain assignment; the
zero-g fixture's primary mass-0 gravitating anchor worked without the
plan's fallback; one test needed several ticks at scale 512 (not one) to
force a warp exit given the 300 s step and the 512 warp-out scale's 128
s-per-tick advance; the divergence suite reads the warp lane through the
live publish/export path rather than the exit path, a stronger anti-vacuity
proof than the plan sketched; fixture ordering shows moon-perturbed
diverging most, not the transfer case as anticipated.

## Phase 52: Rotational Integration & Input Channel — ✅ 2026-07-21

**Goal:** wire live player input to attitude and RCS control — step the Phase
48 rigid-body kernel live in the worker (replacing the Phase 51 static
quaternion), drive it and 6-direction RCS translation through the Phase 50
mixer from a tick-stamped input channel immune to render-rate jitter, add
kill-rot, and resolve the flagged attitude/orbital subcycling rate.

**What it delivered:**
- The attitude/orbital subcycling rate, flagged open since Phase 51: a
  craft-control-gated nested integer divisor inside the existing
  `run_due_steps` drain accumulator — no independent clock. `N = kAttTicksPerStep
  = 16384` (2¹⁴), `kAttDt = 300/16384 = 75·2⁻¹² s ≈ 18.31 ms`, an exact
  binary64 dyadic chosen to clear a 10–20 ms input-latency budget at
  `time_scale 1`; the 16384 fine subtractions telescope binary-exactly to one
  `step_dt`, so the orbital math-lock suite's bit-identity is unaffected and a
  craft-absent or thrust-only run keeps the byte-identical pre-52 loop
- `attitude_tick`: consume tick-stamped input → per-axis ramp (`kInputRampPerTick
  = 2⁻⁴`, ~293 ms to full) → a B-derived max-torque envelope → the live
  Phase 50 `rcs_mix` → the live Phase 48 `rigid_body_step`; 6-direction
  RCS accumulates Δv in a sidecar and injects at the existing Phase 51 thrust
  half-kick seam with a binary-exact half-split, no second orbital-
  state write site
- `InputRing` — a tick-stamped SPSC ring (rigtorp acquire/release discipline);
  `AxisCommand` carries full axis-state (never a delta) stamped to the
  attitude tick it takes effect at; replay of the identical trace at three
  different tick chunkings (including a pathological alternating one) is
  byte-identical across every published field by construction
- Kill-rot: max-decel torque at the mixer envelope, with a guarded
  exact-final-tick commit to literal `ω == 0.0` — the Phase 48 KDK order
  leaves an O(dt²) residual (measured 6.776e-21 rad/s, bounded at 5.0e-20)
  that gets asserted-then-snapped, never silently zeroed; engage-until-done,
  pilot-wins (manual input cancels the latch)
- Exact free-rigid-body attitude propagation through warp via the van
  Zon–Schofield theta-function form — no rotation clamp, a tumble (including
  the Phase 48 Dzhanibekov IC) survives warp with correct flip dynamics; a
  near-separatrix nome (`q > kFrbQMax = 0.5`) hard-stops loudly rather than
  coasting on a NaN attitude. Warp input is physically inert with a ~1s
  hold-to-exit reusing the Phase 51 mid-warp latch verbatim
- Determinism (`nbody_force.cpp`) byte-untouched; worker-TU libm baseline stays
  `{cos, sqrt}`; TSan lane on the input ring reports zero data races; full
  suite **847/847 Release**, confirmed unchanged by the following day's
  verification pass

**Key decisions:** a nested integer divisor over an independent attitude
accumulator — the dyadic-exactness proof is what makes it safe for the
orbital math-lock suite; attitude-before-body ordering on the boundary
tick with RCS Δv injected at the existing kick seam rather than a new write
site; RCS consumes no propellant in M1.1 — a user disposition, not a
unilateral call, deferred to the part-based-craft milestone; the van
Zon–Schofield theta form chosen over the Celledoni Pi form specifically to
keep the hot warp-step path on the engine's existing transcendental family;
a guard-only (not sech/tanh) near-separatrix policy, named for the Phase 54
gate.

**Deviations:** the craft-control-present gate (RCS present) was substituted
for the research skeleton's bare craft-presence flag — a Phase 51
thrust-only craft fixture has no inertia/RCS tensor and would NaN under the
skeleton's gate; the fix preserves every Phase 51 lock untouched. A blocking
edge case in the warp arm: the fine/quantum step values were read before the
tier-switch prologue, so a controlled craft's warp *entry* (which fires
inside that prologue) would run the attitude subcycle during warp — no
prior fixture had driven a controlled craft into warp, so this was latent
until Phase 52's own tests exercised it; fixed by moving the read after the
prologue.

## Phase 53: Telemetry & Debug HUD — ✅ 2026-07-23

**Goal:** let the player see the craft's flight state live and watch thrust
visibly change the orbit — a debug-tier HUD (wet mass, fuel, throttle, attitude
rates, velocity, plus TWR and remaining Δv), a before→after osculating-element
proof that thrust changed the orbit, and HUD / camera / trail correctness across
a floating-origin shift occurring mid-burn. Also seed a craft into the demo
binary (the Phase 52 scaffold was craft-less), add a placeholder attitude triad,
craft camera focus and orbit-fit, and scroll-wheel zoom.

**What it delivered:**
- Burn-boundary latch: the worker publishes the craft's exact (r, v) at the
  ignition and cutoff instants of the most recent burn, each paired with the
  reference body's state from the *same* publish, so the before→after element
  proof is exact by construction rather than resampled after the fact. The edge
  publish already carries ~316 m/s of Δv, so the latch reads a one-publish
  shadow; pairing against the next step's reference instead would skew it by
  ~8,900 km of Earth motion against a 6,778 km orbit radius. Per-burn re-latch
  invalidates the prior after-state; craft-absent latch defaults are locked
- `craft_hud` pure kernel: every number the HUD shows comes out of one
  unit-tested translation unit — Earth-keyed osculating elements from a single
  read-only call into the locked Kepler extractor, TWR and remaining Δv from
  closed forms sharing their transcendental with the locked thrust kernel, three
  fixed-width text blocks whose geometry cannot shift, and a greppable `[craft]`
  stderr line beside an untouched `[telemetry]` line
- HUD render integration: a pure snapshot→`CraftHudState` adapter draws three
  non-overlapping blocks inside the existing batched text pass; live, pre-burn
  and post-burn orbits are each keyed to the reference body's same-instant
  state. The whole surface sits behind an additive setter, so `main.cpp` never
  changed and the shipped binary stays byte-identical to its pre-53 self
- Craft trail, conic, triad and camera geometry: the trajectory draws from a
  float64 authoritative store a floating-origin shift cannot touch — the float32
  GPU buffer is re-derived every frame from the current snapshot offset, so the
  re-derivation error is one narrowing forever instead of a random walk in shift
  count (measured 0.496338 m over 100 shifts, against 8163.28 m if stored
  Sun-relative). Trail, Earth-keyed conic readout and camera all carry bit-level
  invariance locks across a real origin shift
- Wrong-primary craft-warp guard: an opt-in per-craft warp-primary declaration;
  when the global dominance scan disagrees, the worker hard-stops with a
  value-bearing error naming both bodies, their mu and the sim time, before the
  conic machinery can consume it. The real fix — per-craft local-dominant conic
  selection — is a named M1.2 blocker. An empty declaration is structurally
  inert, so every Phase 51 and 52 warp fixture stays bit-identical
- Flyable demo: a 400 km circular low-Earth-orbit craft rides a feel-only slot
  declaring Earth as its warp primary, so the guard is armed in the shipped
  binary rather than sitting inert. One additive setter lights the flight, orbit
  and status blocks, the trail, the predicted conic and the attitude triad; `C`
  fits the camera to the craft's *current* orbit by re-deriving apoapsis from
  the live publish on every press; scroll-wheel zoom joins the camera idiom. The
  whole craft surface hides behind one `if constexpr` toggle, which makes "inert
  without a craft" an executable measurement rather than an argument
- Glyph coverage and vertex budget: `glyph_for` went from a 26-case switch to a
  95-entry constexpr printable-ASCII table (prior rows byte-locked, range check
  made load-bearing); the text vertex budget became the product of four named
  constants rather than a hand-counted literal, deriving 368,640 vertices
- Deterministic force kernel byte-untouched; worker libm baseline unchanged;
  craft-absent runs byte-identical; full suite **882/882 Release** at close,
  re-verified through the 53.3 full re-fly at **903/903**

**Key decisions:** an exact-by-construction before/after latch over resampling
after the fact; the opt-in warp guard plus a named M1.2 blocker rather than
reopening gated Phase 51 warp-conic machinery; the render side hardcodes Earth
as the reference body and never calls the dominance scan, because that scan
ranks by mass and returns the Sun — which outweighs Earth 333,000-fold while
Earth out-pulls it 1,463-fold at a 400 km altitude; an absolute-space float64
trail store re-derived every frame rather than a delta-patched float32 one,
since per-patch rounding random-walks with shift count; an additive setter so
`main.cpp` and the craft-absent binary stay byte-identical.

**Deviations:** Phase 53 FAILED its guided fly-through on 2026-07-23. Automatic
checks and every code-level lock passed at 882/882, but flying the demo surfaced
control and interface defects the locks cannot see: the demo launched at 256×,
so throttle drained propellant at 256× (600→300 kg near-instantly), the ramp
keys popped instead of ramping, the trail read as straight-line chords, the
craft facing was unreadable, and the camera snapped on refit. No engine-physics
defect — the kernels stayed unit-locked and the force kernel untouched. Three
decimal fix phases were inserted before Phase 54 — 53.1 (control policy and
attitude indicator), 53.2 (trail smoothing), 53.3 (camera easing) — and the full
re-fly closing 53.3 re-verified all three of Phase 53's original criteria plus
the Phase 52 attitude and RCS visual carry-over.

so the Phase 53 fly-through — and the deferred Phase 52 attitude/RCS carry-over

failed.

## Phase 53.2: Trail Interpolation at Accelerated Time — ✅ 2026-07-23 (INSERTED)

**Goal:** kill the straight-line polygon the Phase 53 fly-through hit. The craft
past-track trail stores one sample per published frame, so at `step_dt = 300 s`
and 128× each published step is a large arc and the trail is inherently chorded.
Smooth the trail geometry at draw time without touching the locked store. Second
of the three inserted fix phases.

**What it delivered:**
- `craft_trail_spline`, a new pure geometry kernel mirroring the existing triad
  and conic kernels: `std::span` in, `std::span` out, returns the count written,
  `noexcept`, with no render, snapshot or I/O dependency. It subdivides each
  stored chord into `K = 16` sub-points along a centripetal Catmull-Rom spline
  (α = 0.5, Barry–Goldman pyramidal blend, a zero-knot-interval guard, duplicated
  first and last phantom control points). Centripetal parametrisation avoids the
  cusps and self-intersections a uniform spline forms at the wide 300 s chord
  spacing
- The store was tuned for a position stream that does not exist: its 30 km
  minimum-travel gate assumed roughly 1,420 samples per low-Earth orbit, but
  craft position publishes once per 300 s step, so a 5,554 s orbit yields about
  18 samples and every chord cuts a ~98 km sagitta. The gate never fires and was
  never what limited resolution
- Exact control-point pass-through: each stored sample is emitted verbatim, so
  the smoothed curve interpolates every true historical point and still bends
  faithfully through a burn boundary — only the in-between curve is interpolated
- A load-bearing buffer cap: `n` source points produce `(n-1)·K + 1` outputs, so
  the kernel splines only the most-recent points that fit and drops the oldest
  (source is newest-first). The write can never pass the output end and the
  trail region never overflows; a `static_assert` pins `K ≥ 1`. A full store of
  125 source points yields 1,985 vertices with no mid-frame reallocation
- Colour split: the trail became a calm cyan, distinct from the magenta conic,
  so the post-burn divergence between historical trail and live conic stays
  readable
- Render-only: the float64 trail store, its minimum-segment gate and its
  one-sample-per-warp-frame lock are byte-untouched; the force kernel and
  `step_dt = 300` untouched; full suite **893/893 both lanes**

**Key decisions:** spline the trail smooth rather than merely dim it — a
dim-only first pass was rejected on sight; a draw-time subdivision of whatever
the store holds means no locked-store change; centripetal α = 0.5 over uniform
to avoid cusps at the 300 s spacing; K = 16. The test bound is self-calibrating,
requiring the spline to sit four times closer to the true arc than the chords it
replaced, so it cannot pass by accident on a straighter orbit.

**Deviations:** none — implemented exactly to plan. Full per-sample
orbital-element fidelity across burns, which would subdivide each segment along
its own historical osculating conic, needs a locked-store change and is a
documented M1.2 deferral alongside craft-orbital subcycling. The spline is
faithful enough: it passes through every true historical point, and the
interpolated in-between curve on a long-ago pre-burn arc is never seen at the
craft.

## Phase 53.3: Camera Stability & Full Re-Fly — ✅ 2026-07-23 (INSERTED)

**Goal:** two parts. (A) Camera smoothing — zoom and focus were set instantly,
so every orbit refit, recenter and zoom keypress was a hard jump, the snap the
Phase 53 fly-through reported; ease zoom and focus recenter, render-only. (B)
The full Phase 53 re-fly, folding in the Phase 52 attitude and RCS visual
carry-over — the verification that closes the 53.1→53.3 fix chain and re-verifies
Phase 53 itself. Last of the three inserted fix phases before the Phase 54 gate.

**What it delivered:**
- Eased zoom: `zoom_factor_` stays the keybind-set target while a new
  `zoom_current_` advances one `exp_approach` step toward it per rendered frame,
  and the render transform reads `zoom_current_`. It is seeded equal to the
  target at construction, so there is no first-frame glide
- Eased focus recenter: a new `center_residual_`, a `Vec3f64` DIFFERENCE and
  therefore invariant to a floating-origin shift. On a focus change the residual
  is *folded* rather than overwritten, so a focus change mid-ease stays
  continuous; the render computes `effective_center = primary_pos +
  center_residual_` and decays the residual with the same factor. The old and new
  centres can be 10¹¹ m apart, where binary32 has a 16,384 m ULP, which is why
  the residual is float64
- Deferred capture: the focus-change entry points record the outgoing slot and a
  pending flag, and the next `render()` reads *both* old and new focus-body
  positions from the one fresh snapshot and folds the delta — a single-frame,
  origin-shift-invariant difference that avoids a second snapshot copy off the
  render path
- `kCameraEaseFactor = 0.18f`, shared by the zoom approach and the residual
  decay: a one-pole low-pass in error form, `e_n = (1−k)^n · e_0`, giving a time
  constant near 5 frames — about 90% in 12 frames, 95% in 15, roughly 0.2–0.25 s
  at 60 fps. The filter is *not* frame-rate independent: `k` is per rendered
  frame with no `dt` term, so the glide is faster on a faster machine. Pure
  `exp_approach` and `exp_decay` helpers are unit-tested for monotonic
  non-overshooting approach, bounded convergence, an exact-at-target no-op and
  the clamp on k
- The origin-shift re-anchor path is left untouched — it was already continuous
  and was never the snap source
- Full re-fly at 128×: all four groups pass — the camera glides on refit,
  recenter and zoom with no snap; live HUD readouts; thrust grows the orbit with
  a smooth conic and spline trail through a clean mid-burn origin shift; and the
  Phase 52 carry-over of rotation, kill-rot damping and balanced RCS
  translation. Phase 53's three original criteria are visually confirmed, so
  **Phase 53 passes**
- Render-only: the force kernel, the worker translation units and `step_dt = 300`
  byte-untouched; the origin-shift locks green; craft-absent byte-identity
  preserved; the 53.1 attitude indicator and 53.2 spline trail intact; full suite
  **903/903 both lanes**

**Key decisions:** split rendered state from target, so the keybinds set the
target and the rendered value eases toward it; a frame-invariant residual
DIFFERENCE for the focus recenter, so an origin shift mid-ease introduces no
discontinuity; deferred capture of the focus-change delta, reading both slots
from the one fresh snapshot inside `render()`; leave the origin-shift re-anchor
path untouched.

**Deviations:** none on the implementation. One observation logged rather than
treated as a defect: the amber prograde marker draws craft velocity minus Earth
velocity, which is true low-Earth-orbit prograde and tangent to the 92-minute
orbit. At the wide default heliocentric zoom that tangent can point roughly
sunward at points in the orbit, reading as a yellow line toward the Sun;
centering on the craft shows it clearly as tangent. Logged as optional
zoom-polish — hide or scale the marker at extreme zoom-out — and not blocking,
because the direction is correct.
