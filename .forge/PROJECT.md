## What

Official website, devblog, press kit and community hub for Interstellar Engine
and Setare Aerospace: the permanent, linkable home of every devblog post and the
public face of the project. Astro 7 static site, live on GitHub Pages, deploys
on every push to `main`.

Status: active, steady-state content ops. v1.0 shipped 2026-08-11.

Activation milestone: m1.x website slice (D-H), closed at v1.0. Activated
2026-07-13 when m1.x opened (D-I); v1.0 was built over 4 GSD phases (see
`.forge/archive/gsd/MILESTONES.md`).

## North star

Be the permanent, linkable public home of every devblog post and of the
project's public face.
