## Patterns

- Content commits (promoted devlog `.md` files) deploy on push and need no milestone or review ceremony.
- Repo-local context lives in `vault/` (`context.md`, `conventions.md`, `decisions/`); cross-repo decisions live in `../vault/decisions/`.
- `PROMOTE-MANIFEST.md` records which studio drafts are promoted to which paths.
